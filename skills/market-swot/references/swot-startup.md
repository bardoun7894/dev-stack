# SWOT for a startup/app

- S and W come from repo evidence only (cite path): specs done vs open, tests, i18n, shipped screens, pricing strings, DESIGN.md.
- O and T come from market evidence only (cite URL): competitor moves, pricing, reviews (G2, Capterra, store ratings), trends.
- TOWS cross: SO (use strength to take opportunity), ST (use strength against threat), WO (fix weakness to take opportunity), WT (defend/avoid).
- Score every S/W/O/T and every F/R item: impact 1-5 x confidence 1-5 = priority (max 25). Confidence 1-2 needs a second source or is dropped.
- Feature gaps F1..: decision adopt / adapt / skip, one line of reasoning, evidence URL.
